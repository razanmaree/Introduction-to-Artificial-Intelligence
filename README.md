# **Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP)**  
This repository contains an assignment focused on solving the Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP), a combinatorial optimization challenge in the field of artificial intelligence. The goal is to minimize the overall transportation cost for delivering goods or services from a central depot to multiple locations.  

---

## ***Problem Description***  
The Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP) involves optimizing the distribution of goods or services using a fleet of vehicles. The objective is to minimize total delivery costs, considering:  
- **Distance Traveled** – The total distance covered by all vehicles.  
- **Time Traveled** – The total time spent on all routes.  

### Given:  
- **Depot** – Central starting and ending location for the fleet of vehicles.  
- **Locations** – Multiple geographically dispersed destinations requiring delivery.  
- **Transportation Cost** – Includes distance and time expenses.  

### Objective:  
Minimize the total cost of the delivery routes based on:  
- Distance traveled  
- Time traveled  

### Constraints:  
This assignment focuses on a VRP variant **without constraints**, i.e., no vehicle capacity limits or time windows.
